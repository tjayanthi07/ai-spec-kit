## Surprises/Defects
- **Surprise 1:** Users preferred "Remember me" checkbox (not in spec)
  - **Impact:** Low - can be added in v2.0
  - **Action:** Added to backlog for future release
- **Surprise 2:** Password reset emails went to spam for some users
  - **Impact:** Medium - 15% of reset emails not delivered
  - **Action:** Improved email deliverability with SPF/DKIM records
- **Defect 1:** Rate limiting blocked legitimate users during peak hours
  - **Impact:** Medium - 50 users affected in first week
  - **Action:** Increased rate limit from 5 to 10 attempts per 15 minutes
  - **BUG-001:** Rate limit too aggressive for peak hours

## Spec Changes Made
